# FrankX

**Building intelligence that compounds.**

FrankX is [Frank Riemer's](https://github.com/frankxai) home for practical AI architecture, creator tools, education and founder publishing.

The work moves between a design decision, a working artifact and the lesson someone else can use. It connects the Starlight intelligence projects, the GenCreator creative practice and Arcanea's original worlds.

## Work with Frank

For AI architecture, a practical workshop, a focused product prototype or a joint educational demonstration, contact [frank@frankx.ai](mailto:frank@frankx.ai).

Describe who the work serves, the problem and what a useful result would look like.

## Contribution standard

Improve something another person can use. Keep sources and limitations visible, follow each project's license and send changes to its canonical repository where a mirror is present.
